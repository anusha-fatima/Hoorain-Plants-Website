Plant care in four habits

Most plant trouble comes from too much love. These habits keep nearly every plant we sell happy.

Check the soil

Push a finger in an inch. Dry means water; damp means wait.

Find the light

Most of our plants like bright, indirect light, away from harsh midday sun.

Drain well

Never let a pot sit in standing water. Soggy roots are the top killer.

Wipe the leaves

Dust blocks light. A damp cloth every few weeks keeps leaves glossy.
